Show HN: Cortex – Universal AI Memory Layer for Your Codebase

Cortex runs locally, reads your code, and builds a persistent knowledge graph.
Then it feeds that context into Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor — any AI you use.

No cloud. No API keys. Your data never leaves your machine.

What it does:
• Remembers every file, function, and decision across your entire project
• Answers "why did we use Redis?" with the actual commit from 6 months ago
• Reconstructs your mental state: "what was I doing Tuesday at 3pm?"
• Surfaces cognitive debt: "you started a refactor 3 weeks ago and never finished"
• Auto-injects context into Claude/ChatGPT via browser extension

Built in Rust + SQLite. 60 days from idea to working tool.
